## What they sell (customers = farmers, not enterprises)

Grotec manufactures and sells organic/bio agri-inputs for agriculture & horticulture crops:

- **Bio-fertilizers / bio-inoculants:** Azos, Azotob, Rhizob, PHOS, Micromix,
  Bio Jeevan PF, Bio Jeevan TV
- **"Jeevan Sakthi" branded liquid/gel organic growth & protection products:**
  Ultra Action +, Trishul, Asthra, Sanjeevini Gel
- **Other lines:** Raksha, Thavam, Organic Fertilizer (enriched organic manure)
- Positioning: 100% organic, biodegradable, eco-friendly inputs that improve yield, soil
  structure/fertility, pest & disease resistance, and stress tolerance (heat/drought),
  while cutting dependence on chemical fertilizer/pesticide use.

## Vision & mission

- **Vision:** Cultivate long-term, trust-based relationships with farmers through quality
  organic agri-inputs and world-class service.
- **Mission:** Become the industry leader in agriculture/horticulture organic inputs through
