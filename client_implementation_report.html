1 Executive Strategic Overview

GROTEC FarmerOS is an internal enterprise operating system custom-engineered to eliminate operational silos across GROTEC Organic Private Limited. The platform unifies the entire agricultural commerce lifecycle: from high-volume farmer outreach and scientific advisory to factory batch production, warehouse inventory management, vehicle fleet dispatch, mobile field delivery with Proof-of-Delivery (POD), and comprehensive staff attendance and automated payroll.

Key Business Impact: Replaces disconnected spreadsheets, manual dialers, paper delivery challans, and fragmented biometric punches with a single, synchronized platform providing real-time operational truth.

2 Architectural Foundation & Governance

The platform is engineered on an enterprise multi-tenant foundation running PostgreSQL on Supabase, a modular NestJS API core, and a high-performance React Single Page Application.

3 Detailed Functional Delivery: The 12 Live Modules

Module 1: Executive Command Center & Dashboards

100% Live

Centralized operational telemetry giving leadership real-time visibility into outreach volume, conversion velocity, and team targets.

Visual Call Donut: Real-time completion gauge measuring dialed, connected, and completed calls against daily targets.
7-Day Call Pulse: Historical daily histogram tracking organization-wide telecalling volume across the preceding week.
Dynamic Leaderboard: Gamified rankings filterable by Today, Week, and Month recognizing top-performing telecallers.
Role Viewports: Automatically personalizes UI for Founders (strategy), Managers (approvals), and Agents (work queues).

Module 2: Farmer Master Directory & 360° Registry

100% Live

Proprietary agricultural customer relationship management establishing a permanent, deduplicated database of farmers and landholdings.

Unique Farmer IDs: Automated issuance of sequential identification codes (e.g. G-FARM-XXXXX).
E.164 Phone Deduplication: Strict international phone validation preventing duplicate farmer records upon entry.
Agronomic Land Profiles: Captures acreage, soil classification (Black, Red, Clay, Sandy), and irrigation types.
Farmer 360° Profile: Tabbed ledger consolidating demographics, line-item order invoices, crop parcels, and past call notes.

Module 3: High-Velocity Telecaller Workstation

100% Live

Purpose-built telecalling cockpit maximizing agent calling capacity, call note accuracy, and scheduled follow-up compliance.

6 Calling Modes: Direct SIM, Exotel IVR, Active Telecaller, Preview Dialer, Progressive Dialer, and Inbound IVR.
Acoustic Headset Feedback: Subtle audio frequency cues confirming mute/unmute status for headset reassurance.
Strict 3-Way Outcomes: Standardized logging (Interested / Not Interested / Not Answered) decoupled from Next Action.
Mid-Call Quick Edit: Update crop acreages, soil types, or secondary phone numbers on the fly without leaving the active call.

Module 4: Crop Science Knowledge Base & AI Agronomist

100% Live

Empowers agents with scientifically verified advisory protocols for cash crops, linking problems directly to Grotec organic solutions.

Seeded Crop Matrix: Verified protocols for Paddy, Sugarcane, Cotton, Banana, Grapes, Groundnut, Mango, and Pulses.
Exact Product Dosages: Direct application formulas for Bio Jeevan PF/TV, Asthra, Trishul, Sanjeevini Gel, and Ultra Action+.
Problem Categorization: Curated taxonomy covering Pests, Diseases, Nutrient Deficiencies, and Yield Enhancers.
In-Call AI Assistant: Natural language query drawer allowing agents to ask questions mid-call and receive instant dosage answers.

Module 5: Sales Order Booking & Commercial Invoicing

100% Live

Converts interested farmers directly into booked commercial orders, eliminating friction between call centers and warehouses.

Multi-Product Entry: Fast line-item picker with real-time unit pricing and quantity calculation.
Agricultural Payment Terms: Configurable support for Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI transfer, and Advance deposits.
Commercial Invoicing: Automatically generates delivery slips and tax-ready invoices with 1-click browser printing.
Purchase History Sync: Every completed order permanently links to the farmer's 360° profile.

Module 6: Warehouse Inventory & Finished-Goods Ledger

100% Live

Real-time inventory ledger tracking stock across logistical stages, preventing stockouts and shrinkage.

4-State Tracking: Segregates stock into Available, Allocated for Dispatch, In-Transit on Vehicles, and Damaged.
Audited Stock Ledger: Permanent history of all additions, allocations, dispatches, and warehouse adjustments.
Authorized Adjustments: Manager modal for physical stocktaking adjustments with mandatory reason logging.
Category Segmentation: Organized by Bio-Fungicides, Bio-Pesticides, Plant Nutrients, and Yield Boosters.

Module 7: Factory Production Batches & Formulation Register

100% Live

Certifies manufacturing output at the factory level, maintaining formulation traceability from vat to vehicle.

Batch Code Registry: Auto-generates certified batch numbers with production dates and formulation notes.
Output Accounting: Tracks total units manufactured versus remaining balance as orders deplete the batch.
Quality Compliance: Centralized log for batch quality checks, laboratory releases, and packaging validations.
Inventory Auto-Credit: Completing a production run automatically adds available stock to the central warehouse ledger.

Module 8: Fleet Dispatch Manifests & Vehicle Trip Logistics

100% Live

Organizes rural dispatching into structured delivery runs with driver assignments and gate verification checklists.

Route Manifests: Clusters confirmed orders into geographic delivery routes by district and taluk.
Resource Allocation: Assigns delivery vehicles (registration, payload capacity), drivers, and helpers.
Gate Verification Checklist: Pre-departure sign-off verifying physical package counts and carton seals.
Odometer Auditing: Captures vehicle start and closing odometer readings to monitor fuel efficiency and transit time.

Module 9: Mobile Field Delivery Portal & Digital Proof-of-Delivery

100% Live

Mobile-optimized field workstation empowering delivery drivers with digital route sheets and touchless delivery confirmation.

Smartphone Optimized: Lightweight responsive interface designed for field logistics in low-bandwidth areas.
Turn-by-Turn Navigation: 1-tap Google Maps integration and direct farmer click-to-call button.
Digital POD Capture: Touchscreen recipient signature capture and Cash/UPI payment collection verification.
Field Lead Capture: Enables delivery drivers to register neighboring interested farmers as new CRM leads from the road.

Module 10: Operations Exception & Discrepancy Resolution Centre

100% Live

Instant discrepancy resolution handling partial farmer acceptance or transit damage without accounting discrepancies.

In-Flight Exception Logging: Driver submits immediate quantity adjustments directly from the field delivery screen.
Manager Adjudication Queue: Centralized dashboard for operations managers to approve or reject adjustments with notes.
Automated Recalibration: Approving an exception automatically recalculates the order invoice to the revised amount.
Vehicle Stock Reconciliation: Rejected or damaged stock is automatically returned to vehicle inventory without manual entries.

Module 11: Enterprise HRMS & Workforce Management Suite

100% Live

Comprehensive human resources management linking staff attendance, leave quotas, and KPI metrics directly to payroll.

Staff Directory & Vault: Employee profiles with reporting hierarchy and encrypted compliance document storage (Aadhaar, PAN).
Biometric Attendance: Daily punch registers, shift calculations, late entry flags, and eSSL biometric hardware sync.
Leave Management: Quotas for Casual, Sick, and Earned Leaves with employee self-service and manager approval chains.
Automated Payroll Engine: Computes Basic, HRA, statutory deductions, attendance loss-of-pay, and advance recoveries.
Salary Advance Ledger: Disburses employee advances and automatically recovers installments from monthly payroll runs.
Weighted KPI Engine: Combines call volume, talk time, and punctuality into automated monthly scorecards with coaching notes.

Module 12: Business Intelligence, Analytics & Audit Trail

100% Live

Comprehensive operational reporting and security governance providing immutable traceability across all platform events.

Operational Reports: Detailed reports for Agent Leaderboards, Call Activity Logs, Follow-Up Calendars, and Farmer Demographics.
1-Click CSV Exports: Instant client-side CSV downloads on every report for spreadsheet modeling and board presentations.
Immutable Audit Trail: Permanent security log capturing actor ID, timestamp, entity affected, and action details for every mutation.
Role-Based Analytics: Narrows data visibility strictly by organizational hierarchy and departmental permissions.
